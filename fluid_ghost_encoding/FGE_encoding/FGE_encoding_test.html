👻 Encoding with Your Friendly Neighborhood Ghost

Author: Robert Alexander







What this demonstrates:


Arbitrary integer values can be encoded into the 9 non-physical ghost modes of a D3Q19 Lattice Boltzmann simulation and recovered exactly after a full MRT collision cycle. Each ghost channel has a characteristic LCD (least common divisor) that determines its bit capacity. Values are injected by multiplying by the LCD, and recovered by dividing with a rounding offset of LCD/2. With value-dependent pre-correction for systematic readback bias, all 9 channels achieve exact round-trip fidelity across their full value range. This proves that ghost modes can function as lossless per-cell storage within an active LBM simulation, using zero additional memory.


Baseline: Conventional LBM implementations treat ghost modes exclusively as numerical damping artifacts. No prior system is known to encode application data in ghost modes. This test verifies the foundational encoding mechanism of Fluid Ghost Encoding: 85 bits of metadata per cell, stored inside existing distributions, mathematically guaranteed not to affect density, momentum, or stress.
