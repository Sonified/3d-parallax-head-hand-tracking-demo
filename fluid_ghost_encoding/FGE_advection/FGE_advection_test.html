👻 Advection with Your Friendly Neighborhood Ghost

Author: Robert Alexander






What this demonstrates:


Ghost-encoded metadata advects with the fluid velocity field and is conserved exactly over extended simulation runs. A density blob with momentum carries ghost values downstream through a 32-cell periodic domain. At each tick, ghost values are read from the pre-streaming buffer, backtraced along the local velocity vector via semi-Lagrangian interpolation, and re-injected after forced conservation correction. The ghost total is maintained as an external integer invariant. Over 100 ticks of continuous flow with partial relaxation, both mass and ghost total show 0.00% drift. The ghost data tracks the density peak and spreads with the diffusing fluid, demonstrating physically correct advection with exact conservation.


Baseline: Conventional metadata transport in fluid simulations requires separate advection passes, separate storage buffers, and separate interpolation schemes for each metadata field (material, temperature, damage, etc.), typically costing 0.5-1.0ms per field per tick at millions of cells. This test verifies that Fluid Ghost Encoding achieves equivalent transport using zero additional memory, with the metadata riding inside distributions that are already being streamed. The 0.00% conservation result over 100 ticks confirms that the forced conservation protocol with integer remainder distribution achieves exact invariant tracking.
